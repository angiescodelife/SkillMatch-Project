Privacy policy

Your data, in your control

SkillMatch is a final-year university project. This page describes how this application handles your information.

What we store

  • Your account details: name, email, university and account type.
  • Profile information you add: education, headline, skills and career preferences.
  • Your uploaded resume file and the text extracted from it.
  • Calculated match results, saved opportunities and applications you start.

How it's used

Your information is used only to calculate your matches, skill gaps and recommendations inside SkillMatch.

Your resume

Resumes are stored outside the public web folder and can only be opened by you while signed in. You can delete your resume at any time from your profile; skills you have already confirmed stay on your profile until you remove them.

Questions

Contact the project team through your university if you have questions about your data.
